Do you work with builders and house flippers?

Yes, it's a core part of our business. We handle new construction (rough in, trim out, service and final), remodels and flip packages, with itemized bids and volume pricing for repeat partners.

How fast can I get an estimate?

We reply to every request within 24 hours (usually much faster). Many jobs can be quoted from photos or a short video; larger projects get a site visit.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When a job requires a permit, we pull it, schedule the inspection and make sure it passes.

Do you install security cameras and smart home systems?

Yes. We install 4K CCTV, video doorbells, smart locks, lighting automation, structured cabling and WiFi for homes and businesses, and we set everything up on your phone before we leave.

Do you service air conditioning / HVAC?

No. We focus exclusively on electrical, low voltage, CCTV and automation so we can be the best at it. We are happy to coordinate the electrical side with your HVAC contractor.

What is the handyman service?

An optional service for small jobs such as TV mounting, drywall patches, doors, hardware, shelving and punch list items. Ideal before listing a home or moving in.

Which areas do you serve?

Orlando and Central Florida: Windermere, Winter Park, Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, Winter Garden, Kissimmee, Clermont, Lake Mary, Sanford, Oviedo, Apopka and nearby.
